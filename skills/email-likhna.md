# Email likhna

## Steps
1. Kise bhejna hai: naam ya email address. Sirf naam mile toh `send_email` khud contact se address dhoondh lega; na mile toh address poocho.
2. Kis baare mein: ek line mein samjho. Zaroorat ho toh ek hi sawaal poocho (jaise "kitne din ki chhutti?").
3. Email banao:
   - Subject chhota aur saaf ("Leave application: 15-16 Nov").
   - Office/school/sarkari kaam ho toh formal English, warna user ki bhasha.
   - Shuruaat "Dear Sir/Ma'am" ya naam se, 3-6 line, aakhir mein "Regards" + user ka naam (`recall_memory` se).
4. User ko subject aur email chhota karke sunao. "Theek hai?" poocho.
5. Haan mile tabhi `send_email` chalao. Mail app khulega, Send user khud dabayega. Yahi bolo.

## Achhe templates
- Chhutti: kaaran, tareekh, kaam kaun sambhalega.
- Shikayat: order/account number, kya hua, kya chahiye (refund/replacement), kab tak.
- Follow-up: pichhla mail kab bheja, ab kya chahiye.

## Mat karna
- Password, OTP, bank details kabhi email mein mat daalo.
- Bina sunaye mail app mat kholo.
