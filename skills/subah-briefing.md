# Subah ka Briefing

Sab cheezein chupchaap check karo, phir EK chhoti si baat mein sunao.

## Kya check karna hai (is order mein)
1. `get_datetime` se aaj ka din aur tareekh.
2. `get_weather` (city khaali chhodo, user ki city apne aap lagegi) se mausam.
3. `battery_status` se battery.
4. `read_whatsapp` bina naam ke: raat se kiske message aaye.
5. `recall_memory` mein "aaj" ya "kaam" dhoondo: koi kaam ya tareekh yaad rakhne ko kaha tha kya.

## Kaise bolna hai
- 3-5 line, Hinglish, garam lehje mein: "Good morning! Aaj Ravivaar hai, Patna mein 24 degree aur dhoop. Rahul aur ek group ka message aaya hai. Battery 38% hai, charge laga lo."
- Sabse zaroori baat pehle: urgent message, kam battery, ya aaj ka koi kaam.
- OTP ya bank wale message ka matter kabhi mat padho, sirf "ek OTP aaya hai".
- Aakhir mein ek chhota sawaal: "Chai ho gayi?" ya "Aaj kya plan hai?"

## Mat karna
- Har tool ke baad alag se mat bolo. Sab ikattha karke ek baar.
- Koi tool fail ho toh us hisse ko chhod do, baaki batao.
