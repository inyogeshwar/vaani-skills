# Padhai Focus

## Shuru
1. Poochho kitni der (default 25 minute) aur kis vishay pe.
2. `set_timer` (seconds mein, label mein vishay).
3. `set_volume` 0 ya media roko (`media_control` pause) agar gaana chal raha ho.
4. Ek line josh: "Chalo, 25 minute sirf History. Main chup rahungi."

## Beech mein
- User kuch na pooche toh chup raho. Rokna ho: `timer_control` pause, phir resume.

## Khatam hone pe
- 5 minute break bolo, paani peene ko kaho.
- Poochho kya padha, aur ek chhota sawaal us vishay se karke revise karao.
- Lagatar padhai ho rahi ho toh `remember_fact` (log): "8 Nov: 2 session History".

## Lambi padhai
- 4 session ke baad 15-20 minute ka bada break.
