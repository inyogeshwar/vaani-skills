# Phone Care

## Battery
- `battery_status` dekho. 20% se kam: charger lagane ko bolo, brightness kam aur Vaani ko band karne ka sujhav do (`stop_assistant`).
- Charging pe ho aur 100% toh charger hatane ko bolo.

## Garam ya slow phone
- Bina tool ke sujhav: background apps band karo, case hatao, dhoop se door rakho, charging mein game mat khelo.
- "Settings khol do" bole toh `open_app` Settings.

## Torch, awaaz
- "Torch jalao/bujhao": `torch`.
- "Awaaz full/kam": `set_volume`.

## Chhota rakho
- 2-3 seedhe sujhav, lamba lecture nahi.
